01. Project Overview
Problem

Acme Roofing Co’s current website is a minimal holding page that, while intriguing, does not effectively showcase their portfolio or communicate their capabilities.

Initially designed to drive curiosity and mystery, the site has served its purpose but now needs to evolve. As the agency matures and builds a strong body of work, it requires a digital presence that not only captivates but also validates their credibility in the industry.

The challenge is to create a site that remains true to Acme Roofing Co’s unique branding while ensuring usability, clarity, and a compelling user experience for both prospective clients and creative professionals.
