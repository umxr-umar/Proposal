01. Project Overview
Problem

Acme Roofing Co’s current website is a minimal holding page that, while intriguing, does not effectively showcase their portfolio or communicate their capabilities.

Initially designed to drive curiosity and mystery, the site has served its purpose but now needs to evolve. As the agency matures and builds a strong body of work, it requires a digital presence that not only captivates but also validates their credibility in the industry.

The challenge is to create a site that remains true to Acme Roofing Co’s unique branding while ensuring usability, clarity, and a compelling user experience for both prospective clients and creative professionals.

03
01. Project Overview
Solution

The new website will transform Acme Roofing Co’s online presence into an interactive and immersive portfolio experience. It will retain the agency’s signature blend of digital and analog aesthetics.

Subtle micro-interactions and strategic motion design will enhance the user experience, making the site feel dynamic yet intuitive.

The design will maintain the agency’s distinctive visual identity, leveraging dark charcoal and off-white tones with vivid accent colors to reinforce brand recognition.

04
01. Project Overview
Impact

A refined website will position Acme Roofing Co’s as a forward-thinking creative leader, attracting high-profile clients and top-tier creative talents.

The platform will engage visitors through a balance of curiosity-driven interactivity and clear content presentation, increasing dwell time and exploration.

With a 24/7 digital showcase, the site will streamline client acquisition by reinforcing trust and credibility. This strategic upgrade will ensure Acme Roofing Co’s brand remains as impactful and memorable as the work they create.
